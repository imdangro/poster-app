Social media on autopilot – on your own server

Poster runs 24/7 on your NAS or server and keeps your profiles busy: it follows the news on your topic (RSS feeds, Google News, X), lets AI pick the best story and draft the posts – and publishes them to X and Facebook after your ✅ in Telegram. Got something of your own to say? Write it once – AI adapts it to every platform.

Download free LITE See what PRO adds

LITE is free under the AGPL-3.0 license. Docker on a NAS or a Linux server is all you need.

Install it with one command over SSH:

curl -fsSL https://posterapp.pl/install.sh | sudo bash

How it works

You set the topic and the sources once. Poster does the rest on its schedule.

📰
1 · SOURCES

Fresh news

RSS feeds of news sites, Google News and X profiles. Duplicates and old stories are filtered out.

🤖
2 · AI

Picked and written

Gemini or a model on your own hardware (Ollama) picks the most important story and writes the posts.

✅
3 · TELEGRAM

You approve

The draft with the article photo lands in Telegram. One tap on ✅ publishes it, ❌ rejects it.

🚀
4 · PUBLISHING

Posted

X and Facebook get the post and you get a report. A failed platform is retried with one tap on 🔁.

Write once, post everywhere

Your own news – a promotion, an event, an opinion? Write the post your way and AI adapts it to every platform.

  • A separate version for X (within 280 characters) and for Facebook – AI keeps your facts and adds none
  • A photo straight from your phone's camera and a link at the end of the post
  • Edit the versions, then publish right away or send them for approval in Telegram

A schedule that looks human

Every few hours or at the hours you pick – on the days you choose.

  • Preview of the next runs before you save
  • Random offset of up to 45 minutes, so posts do not go out to the minute
  • Pause, resume and “Run now” in one click

Costs under control

The “Cost of one post” card shows what costs money with the current settings.

  • Images cost nothing – the photo from the article or a post with a link
  • AI text usually fits in a free quota (a Gemini key without billing) or runs on your own hardware
  • The approximate X API cost of a post is shown before you turn publishing on

Every draft archived

Images and texts of all posts stay on your NAS – ready to copy, download or post by hand.

  • Texts for X and Facebook, plus a TikTok script
  • Publishing results with links to the posts
  • Why AI picked the story
🔐

Your keys stay home

API keys and account tokens are stored only in the panel on your NAS. No cloud account, no tracking.

🏠

No open ports

The panel polls Telegram, so the NAS does not have to be exposed to the internet.

📱

English and Polish

The panel, Telegram messages and the docs in both languages. The panel works on a phone too.

FAQ

What do I need to run Poster?

A NAS or a Linux server with Docker and Docker Compose v2, about 1 GB of free RAM and a Telegram bot. On a clean Ubuntu or Debian server the installer sets up Docker itself. The guide uses a UGREEN NAS with UGOS as the example, but any Docker host works.

How do I install it?

Log in to your NAS or server over SSH and run curl -fsSL https://posterapp.pl/install.sh | sudo bash. The installer checks Docker (on Ubuntu or Debian it installs it if missing), downloads Poster, prepares the configuration, starts the containers and shows the panel address with a one-time code for your password – you set everything else in the browser. Prefer to do it by hand? The README has a step-by-step guide.

How much does running it cost?

Poster LITE is free. AI text usually fits in a free quota (a Gemini key from a project without billing) or runs on your own hardware with Ollama. Publishing through the X API is billed by X: in 2026 about USD 0.015 for a post with a photo and about USD 0.20 for a post with a link.

Can I publish my own posts, not only news?

Yes. In New post you write the text (optionally with a link and a photo), AI adapts it to every platform, and you publish right away or after approval in Telegram. LITE posts to X and Facebook, PRO also to Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, a Telegram channel and TikTok. In PRO you can also send the bot /post with your text or a photo in Telegram – the draft comes back with ✅ – and schedule posts once or on repeat, with everything in a content calendar.

Does Poster work with my YouTube channel?

Yes, in PRO. Paste the channel link into a “YouTube channel” bot – Poster checks the public RSS feed of your videos every few minutes, so you need no YouTube login and no API key. Every new video gets a post with its thumbnail and a link on all platforms of the bot.

Do I have to expose my NAS to the internet?

No. The panel polls Telegram for button clicks and talks to the social media APIs with outgoing connections only. For remote access a VPN such as Tailscale is the simplest option.

What happens when the 12 months of PRO updates end?

Your installation keeps working with no time limit. Only updates stop until you renew the licence – with the Renew the license button in the Poster panel (Settings → PRO license), which fills in your key for you, or here. A renewal bought before the end date adds 12 months to it; after expiry you get 12 months from the purchase.

Can I move from LITE to PRO later?

Yes. After the purchase copy the license key from the order page (Access purchase in the email from Polar) and paste it in the panel (Settings → PRO edition) – the panel checks it and shows one command to run over SSH: sudo bash scripts/upgrade-to-pro.sh. PRO uses the same data directory, so your bot, keys and history stay where they are.

Can I get a refund for PRO?

Yes – within 14 days of the purchase, as long as you have not activated the license. Checking the key in the panel does not activate it; upgrade-to-pro.sh does, as it gives you the PRO code – from then on there is no refund without a reason (problems with how Poster works are handled as usual).
